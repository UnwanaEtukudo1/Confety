FAQ

How do I try ticket checkout?

Choose an event, select Get tickets, and place a demo order. Use sample details when trying the checkout.

Where are my demo orders stored?

The prototype saves orders in the browser where you placed them. The organizer workspace has a Ticket orders tab for viewing those records.

How do I create an event?

Choose Create an event on the homepage, then add the date, venue, description, and ticket price.
